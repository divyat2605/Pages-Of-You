Us Forever

February 1st, 2024
I found this old leather-bound diary in a secondhand bookstore today. It’s weirdly in perfect condition, almost like it was waiting for someone to pick it up. The guy at the counter gave me a strange look when I bought it, muttering something about "unfinished stories." I didn’t care. I just liked the way it felt in my hands—heavy, real.

I open it now, pen in hand, and for the first time in years, I feel like writing.

February 5th, 2024

I don’t know why I’m doing this. Maybe because I’ve never been able to say these words out loud. Maybe because I need to get them out before they drown me.

It started with her. It always does, doesn’t it?

The first time I saw her, she wasn’t even looking at me. She was tying her shoelaces in the middle of the hallway, completely oblivious to the rush of people stepping over and around her. I remember thinking, Who even does that? Then, as if she heard my thoughts, she looked up—straight at me.

And smiled.

That was the moment. My moment. The kind you don’t realize is important until later.

February 10th, 2024

We became friends. Not instantly, not dramatically—just in a quiet, natural way. She was all laughter and chaos, and I was... well, not. But somehow, we fit.

She’d text me things like: "If you could be anywhere right now, where would you be?"

And I’d reply with something logical like: "Probably home, it’s comfortable."

Then she’d send, "Wrong answer. The correct answer is: anywhere but home, with me."

I should’ve known then. I should’ve seen the way she was pulling me into her world, piece by piece.

February 14th, 2024

Valentine’s Day.

I wanted to tell her today. That she wasn’t just my best friend. That when she laughed, the world made sense. That her voice was my favorite sound. That I was in love with her.
But before I could, she told me about him.
The guy she liked. The one who made her heart race. And it wasn’t me.
I smiled. I listened. I said all the right things. And later that night, I wrote all the wrong ones.

February 20th, 2024



She doesn’t know. She’ll never know. And maybe that’s for the best.

She calls me her “favorite person.” I wish she knew what that did to me.

February 25th, 2024

She’s happy. That should be enough, right? If you love someone, you want them to be happy—even if their happiness doesn’t include you. That’s what people say.

But people lie.

🌹❤

March 1st, 2024

I saw her today, standing in the rain, laughing like she belonged in it. And suddenly, it hit me:

She’s not mine to love. She never was.

Maybe one day, I’ll meet someone whose laugh feels like home, who looks at me the way I look at her. Maybe.

Until then, I’ll keep writing.

For her. For me. For the love story that never was.

"Some love stories are meant to be felt, not just read. If you found yourself in these pages, maybe this story was never mine alone. Maybe, it was always yours too."
